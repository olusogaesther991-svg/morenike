Meet the person behind the work

I bring brand thinking and practical detail to every Shopify store.

I’m Esther Olusoga, a Shopify store designer focused on creating online shopping experiences that feel clear, considered and true to each brand. I enjoy bringing the pieces of a storefront together — from its overall layout and navigation to the way products are presented.

I look beyond how a store looks. I pay attention to how easily visitors can understand the offer, find what they need and move through the shopping journey. That means thoughtful page structure, useful product details, responsive design and SEO foundations that support discoverability.

Whether you’re starting a new store or improving an existing one, I value clear communication, careful work and an approach shaped around your goals.

A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Some things you may want to know before getting in touch.

Can you help with a brand-new Shopify store?

Yes. I can help plan and set up the core storefront, including its pages, navigation, collections and product presentation.

Can you improve a store that is already live?

Yes. We can identify the areas that need attention, such as layout, product discovery, page clarity or SEO foundations, and agree on a focused set of improvements.

Will my store work on mobile devices?

Responsive presentation is part of the design process, so the storefront is considered for both desktop and smaller screens.

How do we get started?

Send me a message with your store link (if you have one), what you sell and what you would like to improve. We can discuss the next steps from there.

Do you promise a specific sales or Google ranking result?

No. Store design and SEO improvements can support a better customer experience and stronger foundations, but sales and search rankings depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.
